whereAreYou lets you share your location with your people and see theirs on an OpenStreetMap map.

* Circles: every member of a circle automatically sees the others.
* Invite with a QR code or a link, no account or phone number needed.
* End-to-end encryption (AES-256-GCM).
* No central server: encrypted positions go through several public Nostr relays of your choice.
* No dependency on Google services.
* Free software (GPL-3.0).
